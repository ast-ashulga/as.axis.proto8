    en: |-
      The central portion of Tablet V contains a substantial lacuna in which Columns IV and V are entirely lost and the surrounding columns are heavily damaged, rendering the core narrative unrecoverable from the Standard Babylonian Version alone. Scholars reconstruct that this missing material likely encompassed the heroes' approach to Humbaba, the initial confrontation, and the early stages of combat in the Cedar Forest. The damaged upper portions of Columns I and II further obscure what narrative context preceded the lacuna, while Column III breaks off after Enkidu apparently begins interpreting a dream. Hittite and Old Babylonian parallel versions are employed by scholars to supplement and partially reconstruct the sequence of events absent from the Assyrian recension.

Верхние части первого и второго столбцов также серьёзно повреждены, что существенно затрудняет реконструкцию последовательности событий.
