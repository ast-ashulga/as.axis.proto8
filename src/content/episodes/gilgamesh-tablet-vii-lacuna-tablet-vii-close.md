      The closing section of Tablet VII is substantially damaged or lost, leaving the narrative of Enkidu's death and the conclusion of his Underworld vision incomplete in surviving cuneiform witnesses. The text appears to have broken off at the moment when Ereshkigal, queen of the Underworld, raises her head in the vision reported by the dying Enkidu, though the significance of this gesture remains uncertain due to the lacuna. Scholars reconstruct that the missing portions would have included the continuation of the dream narrative and the account of Enkidu's final moments, drawing on parallel passages from other tablet witnesses. The extent of what is irrecoverably lost from this closing section of the tablet cannot be precisely determined from currently available sources.
