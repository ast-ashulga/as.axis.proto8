    en: |-
      Tablet V opens with Gilgamesh and Enkidu standing before the Cedar Forest, gazing upon its towering trees and recognizing it as the sacred dwelling of the gods, described as a shrine associated with the goddess Irnini. During their journey, Gilgamesh receives multiple dreams, which Enkidu interprets as omens of favorable outcome, tentatively identifying a great mountain in the visions with Humbaba himself. The passage is partially damaged but appears to depict the heroes pausing at intervals to rest and perform ritual dream-seeking, with Gilgamesh petitioning a mountain to send him a prophetic vision. A third dream follows, marked by cosmic upheaval — thunder, darkness, and an image scholars associate with a violent encounter with death. Drawing on what is likely a Hittite parallel version, the text then describes Gilgamesh praying to the sun-god Shamash, who responds by unleashing thirteen winds against Humbaba, immobilizing the guardian completely. Overwhelmed, Humbaba surrenders and appeals to Gilgamesh for mercy, offering to serve him as a vassal.

По молитве Гильгамеша солнечный бог насылает на Хумбабу восемь ветров, лишая стража леса возможности двигаться вперёд или отступить. Побеждённый Хумбаба просит о пощаде, предлагая стать слугой героя, однако текст свидетельствует, что Гильгамеш и Энкиду наносят ему смертельные удары. После гибели Хумбабы Энкиду срубает кедры, а герои снимают с поверженного стража оружие и открывают тайное жилище Ануннаков.
