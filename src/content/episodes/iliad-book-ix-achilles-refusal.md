      Одиссей, наполнив чашу вином, обращается к Ахиллесу с речью, излагая катастрофическое положение ахейцев: троянцы разбили лагерь у самых кораблей, Зевс посылает им благоприятные знамения, а Гектор, объятый «могучим безумием», клянётся поджечь флот на рассвете. Речь завершается указанием на то, что даже если Агамемнон ненавистен лично, следует пожалеть остальных ахейцев и добыть себе великую славу, сразив Гектора.
    en: |-
      Odysseus opens the embassy's appeal by acknowledging the abundance of Achilles' hospitality before pivoting to the crisis at hand, warning that the Achaean fleet faces imminent destruction. He describes the Trojan encampment pressing close to the ships, with Hector raging in Zeus-backed confidence and vowing to burn the hulls at dawn. Odysseus then recites the full catalogue of Agamemnon's offered gifts — tripods, gold, horses, women including Briseis, and seven cities — framing them as sufficient recompense for Achilles' injured honour. He invokes Peleus's parting counsel that gentle-mindedness and restraint bring greater honour than stubborn wrath, appealing to Achilles' filial piety. Achilles, however, delivers his celebrated refusal, rejecting Agamemnon's gifts and declaring that honour is equal whether a man fights or withdraws, invoking Thetis's prophecy of his double fate. He announces his intention to sail home at dawn, inviting the embassy to report his answer and urging the Greeks to seek their safety by other means.
