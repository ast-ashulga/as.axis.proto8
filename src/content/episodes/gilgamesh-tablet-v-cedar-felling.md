    en: |-
      Enkidu urges Gilgamesh not to accept whatever proposal or plea Humbaba makes, insisting that the guardian of the Cedar Forest must not be allowed to live. The passage is heavily damaged at this point, with both the Hittite and Assyrian versions breaking off or surviving only in badly mutilated lines, making a complete reconstruction impossible. Despite these lacunae, the text concludes — as scholars reconstruct from the fragmentary final lines — with the successful outcome of the expedition and the severing of Humbaba's head. The segment thus marks the climactic resolution of the heroes' confrontation with the forest guardian, though the precise narrative details of the killing itself remain largely irrecoverable from surviving sources.
