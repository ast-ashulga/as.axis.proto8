# Mnemosyne Engine — Founding Team Roles

Four roles form the core ideation team. Each covers one essential dimension of the project: vision, culture, experience, and technology. At this stage, these four people should be present at every key decision.

---

## Product Lead

**The person who holds the whole.** Responsible for ensuring that cultural integrity, user experience, and technical feasibility converge into a coherent product — and that the team moves forward instead of circling.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain the product vision and north star
- Facilitate ideation sessions and drive decisions to closure
- Translate between domain, design, and engineering perspectives
- Set priorities and manage scope at each phase
- Identify what needs validation and design experiments to test it

### Accountabilities
- The product roadmap and its sequencing
- What gets built in phase one and why
- Alignment across all four roles
- Communication with any external stakeholders or partners

### Expectations
- Comfortable holding ambiguity without collapsing it prematurely
- Able to say no — to features, to scope, to distraction
- Brings structure to conversations without killing creative energy
- Has genuine respect for both the cultural material and the technical constraints

---

## Cultural & Domain Expert / Content Strategist

**The person who knows what these stories actually are.** Responsible for ensuring the platform represents its source traditions with accuracy, depth, and respect — and that editorial decisions reflect that understanding.

### Responsibilities
- Provide deep knowledge of the epic traditions in scope (Mesopotamian, Greek, Roman, Indian, Central Asian, Egyptian, Mesoamerican and others)
- Define what counts as faithful vs. reductive representation of each tradition
- Guide the selection and prioritization of source texts and translations
- Establish the editorial philosophy: what content belongs on the platform, at what depth, and in what form
- Identify where AI-generated content requires human review and where it does not
- Advise on cultural sensitivities and representation risks

### Accountabilities
- Accuracy and integrity of all content on the platform
- The content taxonomy — how traditions, texts, and fragments are categorized and related
- Source selection strategy (which public domain texts, which translations, which scholarly frameworks)
- The line between scholarly rigor and accessible presentation

### Expectations
- Background in comparative mythology, classical studies, or a related humanities field
- Able to collaborate with engineers and designers without losing academic standards
- Understands that accessibility and depth are not opposites
- Willing to define what is "good enough" for phase one rather than waiting for perfection

---

## UX / Creative Lead

**The person who makes it feel right.** Responsible for how users actually experience the platform — from first contact to deep exploration — and for ensuring the interface serves both the newcomer and the scholar.

### Responsibilities
- Own the user experience vision and the "Onion Model" navigation concept
- Define how depth levels work in practice: what the user sees, how they go deeper, what draws them forward
- Design information architecture that reflects the platform's non-hierarchical cultural philosophy
- Translate complex structural relationships (cross-tradition links, narrative layers) into intuitive interfaces
- Ensure visual and interaction language respects the tone of the material

### Accountabilities
- The interface concept and user journey
- How the platform feels to a first-time visitor and to a returning expert
- Visual identity and aesthetic direction
- Usability — that the platform does not require a manual to navigate

### Expectations
- Experience designing content-heavy or knowledge-dense products
- Able to hold the "unifying, not splitting" principle in every design decision
- Comfortable with ambiguity in early phases — can sketch directions before requirements are fixed
- Will challenge product and technical constraints when they conflict with good experience

---

## Technical Lead

**The person who knows what is buildable.** Responsible for technical feasibility across the full stack — web platform, data architecture, AI integration — and for grounding ideation in what can actually be built.

### Responsibilities
- Assess technical feasibility of product and UX concepts during ideation
- Propose and evaluate architectural approaches (linking systems, content storage, AI pipelines)
- Identify technical risks early — before they become constraints
- Define the shape of the engineering team that will eventually need to be hired
- Participate in research into relevant technical frameworks (knowledge graphs, narrative addressing, AI content generation)

### Accountabilities
- Technical direction and architecture decisions
- Feasibility input during ideation — flagging what is realistic in phase one
- The technical vocabulary of the project: how systems are named, described, and communicated
- Identifying where AI can accelerate content work and where it introduces risk

### Expectations
- Broad full-stack competence with genuine interest in AI/ML applications
- Able to reason about data architecture and knowledge representation, not just application code
- Does not over-engineer at ideation stage — can distinguish "what we need now" from "what we might need later"
- Engages seriously with the cultural and editorial dimensions of the project, not just the technical ones
